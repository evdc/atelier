## Context
Spawned children and forks spend money without a person in each loop. Mockup step 7: "Each child takes a slice of
its parent's budget. If a subtree runs over, it pauses and asks you." DESIGN §11.5.

## Scope
- `SessionCreated` gains `#[serde(default)] budget_usd: Option<f64>`. It's set by `spawn { budget }`, or by the
  person on any session through a new `session.set_budget` call that appends `session.budget_set`.
- Projection `trees`:
  - for each session, `tree_cost` = its own cost plus its descendants' costs, from `cc.result.total_cost_usd`
    as already folded into `sessions.cost_usd`;
  - the effective budget = the nearest ancestor-or-self budget.

  It's deterministic: walk parents on each cost change, and depth is ≤ 3.
- **Enforcement** in the supervisor, when a turn ends:
  - if a session's tree is over budget, it doesn't deliver queued agent messages and refuses new `spawn`/`ask`/
    `send` from the tree, with "budget reached: ask the person";
  - it appends `tree.over_budget { root }` once, which shows red, needs you, and notifies (AT-014).

  Raising the budget clears it. Never kill a turn mid-flight.
- A child's budget must fit within what's left of its parent's. `spawn` refuses or clamps it, and says which.
- **Web**: the parent's session header shows *$3.40 of $8.00* as a hairline bar when a budget is set, and the
  inbox has the over-budget item with "raise to…".
